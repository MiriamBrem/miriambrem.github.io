# Investigating Trial Line Burden and Prosecutorial Efficiency in the DeKalb County District Attorney’s Office

This presents a series of analyses conducted for the DeKalb County District Attorney’s Office and offers recommendations for the office. 

The District Attorney’s (DA) Office prosecutes felony cases committed in DeKalb County in Superior Court, handling thousands of cases per year. Our analysis focuses specifically on the Trial Division, which is responsible for prosecuting the majority of felony criminal cases in DeKalb County. In light of the high proportion of cases it prosecutes, evaluating the Trial Line’s burden is critical to ensuring it is able to conduct its work effectively. This paper analyzes prosecutorial performance and presents recommendations.
